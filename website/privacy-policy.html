Privacy Policy

How Root collects, stores, and handles your information as a member of a pilgrimage tour.

Information we collect

To create your account and connect you with your tour group, Root collects:

  • Phone number or email — used to sign in and verify your account
  • Full name
  • Profile photo
  • Bio
  • City or place of origin
  • Phone number visibility preference — your choice of who can see it

Travel-related information

Because Root is built around your trips, it also keeps:

  • Your full trip history — every tour you've joined, kept accessible indefinitely
  • Your group or batch assignment for each trip
  • The participant directory for every group you join — the name, photo, and city of fellow members
  • Your spiritual leader or coordinator's details — photo, bio, and contact information

Content you add

Root also stores what you create and share inside the app:

  • Travel journals — your personal reflections from the trip
  • Photos you upload to your group's shared gallery
  • Admin broadcast notifications sent to your group

System information

To keep the app running smoothly and securely, Root records:

  • Authentication tokens and sessions
  • Sync and cache logs
  • Content access patterns

Where your data lives

Your information is stored on Root's remote application server, in a MariaDB database, and is used exclusively for the Root app.

We do not share your data with third parties. Your information is kept confidential at all times.

How long we keep it

Your trip history is retained permanently — as a member, you keep indefinite access to the details of every past trip and the members who joined you.

If you'd like your account or data deleted, email us and we'll process your request. Administrator access to the system is logged and managed by our server administrator.

Photos you upload

When you upload a photo to Root, its metadata — including EXIF data and geolocation, if present — is preserved as-is. We only change the file's name when it's stored; no metadata is stripped or removed.
