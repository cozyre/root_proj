Everything for the road, in one pocket.

A pilgrimage carries a lot: schedules, hymns, prayers, and the faces of everyone walking with you. Root keeps it close, so you can stay present in the moment instead of digging for a printed page.

Itinerary

See where the group is headed each day, what time to be at the bus, and what's planned at every stop along the way.

Songs & devotions

Open the lyrics and readings for the day's gathering, so everyone can join in without passing around a booklet.

Your fellow travelers

Browse the directory for your group and see who's coordinating the trip, so a familiar face is never far off.

The trip, in pictures

Add your photos to the shared gallery and keep a journal of what the journey meant to you as it unfolds.

Know who's walking with you.

Every trip has its own group, and every group has a directory: names, faces, and the leader who's guiding the way. You'll always know who's beside you, from the first stop to the last.

  • A directory for each tour, kept separate from the rest
  • Photos and cities, so faces are easy to place
  • Your coordinator's contact, always within reach

Bring your whole group along.

Root is built for tour groups planning a pilgrimage together. Download the app to check the itinerary, follow along with devotions, and meet the people you'll be walking with.
